About Me

A Little About Me

I'm Mustafa Shakir, a content creator and video editor from Pakistan.

My journey started with exploring graphic design and video editing. Over time, I developed a deeper interest in content creation, storytelling, and the process of turning ideas into engaging digital experiences.

Since June 2024, I've been creating content for my own platforms while also working with people and organizations across different industries.

I enjoy working across the creative process, whether it's developing a script, finding the right story, producing visuals, or editing the final video.

I'm also pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence at NUST. This gives me an opportunity to explore the technology behind many of the tools I use and understand how AI can contribute to creative work.

Outside client projects, I enjoy experimenting with storytelling, exploring technology, and finding new ways to communicate ideas through content.

I'm still evolving as a creator, and that's what makes the journey interesting.
